Cube Solver v3

How to use

  1. Hold your cube with white on top and green facing you.
  2. In Paint mode, tap a color in the palette then tap stickers to fill. In Tap-cycle mode, tap a sticker repeatedly to cycle colors.
  3. Center stickers are fixed (they define each face's color).
  4. Tap Solve. Compact finds a ~20-move solution. Step-by-step finds the same solution but splits it into smaller phases so you can apply a few moves at a time and double-check your cube.
  5. Use the playback controls to step through the moves.

Move notation

Letters = face turns (Up, Down, Left, Right, Front, Back). A prime mark (') means counter-clockwise. 2 means 180°.

Install to Home Screen

In iOS Safari, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. The app then runs full-screen and works offline.
